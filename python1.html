Barcode Check Digit Solver

Did you know the 13th digit of a barcode is special? It is called a check digit. These digits are calculated using a complicated formula to check for mistakes in the barcode when scanned. Most barcodes use this formula: take every 2nd digit, add them up, and multiply it by 3. Then take the other digits (excluding the check digit of course) and add them up. Add the two sums together and take the last digit. Then do 10 minus what you got before. If the answer is 10 the check digit is 0, if not it is just the answer you get. Put in the first 12 digits of the barcode to find the check digit!
